### Key differences
* **Exterior Styling & Wheels:** Executive, Prestige, and Corporate roll on R17 Diamond-cut alloy wheels [3][6]. Platinum, Signature, and Signature Knight feature larger R18 Diamond-cut alloys [3][6]. The Signature Knight and Lounge Edition feature exclusive black-painted alloy wheels, dark chrome or black painted accents, and unique styling elements like brass/silver inserts [2][3].
* **Safety & ADAS:** Lower variants (Executive, Prestige, Corporate) lack ADAS features, whereas Platinum, Signature, Signature Knight, and Lounge Edition come equipped with Hyundai SmartSense Level 2 ADAS [9]. Signature variants and above also add Surround View Monitor (SVM) and Blind-spot View Monitor (BVM) [9][10].
* **Seating Configurations:** Executive, Prestige, Corporate, and Platinum are offered in a 7-seater format [3][9]. Signature offers both 6-seater (captain seats) and 7-seater layouts [3][9]. Signature Knight is strictly a 6-seater variant [3][9]. The Lounge Edition is uniquely built as a 5-seater configuration [2][12].
* **Infotainment & Extras:** The Executive model lacks an advanced touchscreen navigation system and connected car features [11][12]. The Lounge Edition uniquely includes a 29.46 cm (11.6") touchscreen rear seat entertainment system on the passenger side [11].
